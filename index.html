FAQ

Won't this slow down my site? What about my existing setup?

How long does setup actually take?

About 15 minutes on a call — I configure the widget with your real hours, services, and pricing myself. You don't touch any code.

Will it work with my existing website?

Yes. It's a small piece of code added to your site, regardless of what platform it's built on. It sits on top of what you already have — nothing gets rebuilt or replaced.

Do I need to give up my current booking system?

No. The widget captures the lead and the requested time, and connects to your calendar — it doesn't require you to switch tools.

Is visitor data safe?

Yes. Conversations and leads are tied to your business only — we never share or pool visitor data across other businesses using LeadFlow HQ.

What happens if it can't answer a question?

It sticks to what it knows — your hours, services, and pricing — and hands off to a lead capture instead of guessing. It won't invent answers about your business.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, it's month-to-month after the one-time setup. No contract lock-in.

Can I integrate this with my existing CRM or email platform?

Yes. I can connect it to most major CRMs, email platforms, and calendar systems. During setup, we'll wire it to whatever tools you're already using so leads flow automatically.

Does the widget handle scheduling or just capture leads?

It captures the lead and their preferred time. You handle the actual booking — either manually by calling them back (which is when most jobs get won anyway) or by connecting it to your calendar system for automatic scheduling if you prefer.

What's your policy on data security and privacy?

All conversations and lead data are encrypted and stored securely. Your customer information belongs to you — we never sell it, share it with competitors, or use it for any purpose other than delivering the service.
