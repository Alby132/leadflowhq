What the assistant will and won't do

Answers from your information only

Hours, treatments, prices and policies you give us. If it doesn't know, it says so and takes a message instead of guessing.

No medical advice

It doesn't diagnose or recommend treatment. Questions like that are passed to your team.

Emergencies go to your emergency line

If someone describes pain or an urgent problem, it gives them your emergency number or out-of-hours instructions first.

Your booking system stays

It captures requests. Your team still confirms every appointment, so nothing is double-booked.

Questions clinics ask

Does it book appointments directly into our system?

Not by default. It collects the request and sends it to your team to confirm, which works with any practice management software. If your system supports online booking, we can talk about linking to it.

What happens to patient information?

The assistant only collects what's needed to call the patient back: name, phone number and the reason for the appointment. It's used only to send the request to your clinic. We'll walk you through exactly where it's stored before you decide.

Will it work on our website?

Yes. It's a small script added to your existing site. If someone else manages your website, we can send them the one line to add.

Can patients write in other languages?

Yes. It replies in the language the patient writes in, using your clinic's information.

How long does setup take?

Usually a few days from our first call. Most of that is us writing and testing the answers. Your part is one call and a quick review.
